# 7. User Manual: All in One News

*All your news channels, live streams and useful websites in one place.*

---

## 7.1 Getting started

1. Install **All in One News** from Google Play (Android 7.0 or newer) or the App Store (iOS 15 or newer).
2. Open the app. There is **no sign-up and no account**. You can start straight away.
3. The first time, the app downloads the latest list of channels. After that it opens instantly and also works with a weak connection, showing the last list it downloaded.

## 7.3 Opening a website or channel

* **Tap a card** to open it.
* Depending on the channel it opens in one of these ways:
  * **A browser tab on top of the app.** Close it with ✕ or the back gesture to return.
  * **The app's own browser screen**, with a toolbar: ◀ back, ▶ forward, ⟳ reload, ⌂ start page. Use 🔖 at the top to add the channel to your favourites, and ⋮ to share the link or open it in your normal browser.
  * **Your phone's browser or the channel's own app**, if installed.
* Videos and live streams play inside the page; tap the full-screen button in the video player for full screen.

## 7.4 Categories

* Use the **tabs** (News, Live Streaming, Sports…) to see only one kind of channel. **All** shows every category.
* On the **All** tab each category shows its first cards; tap **See all** for the complete list.

## 7.5 Searching

Tap **Search** (bottom bar or the search box on Home) and type part of a name, a topic or a web address, for example *cricket*, *weather* or *bbc*. Results appear as you type. With an empty search box you can jump straight into a category.

## 7.6 Favourites (your own bookmarks)

* **Add:** press and hold any card → **Add to favourites**, or tap 🔖 in the app's browser screen.
* **Open:** bottom bar → **Favourites**.
* **Reorder:** drag the ☰ handle.
* **Remove:** swipe the item to the left (tap **Undo** if it was a mistake), or press and hold a card → **Remove from favourites**.

Favourites are stored **only on your phone**.

## 7.7 More actions on a card

Press and hold any card to see its description and these options: **Open**, **Add/Remove favourite**, **Share**, **Open in browser**.

## 7.8 Getting the latest channels

* The list updates automatically when you open the app.
* **Pull down** on the Home screen to refresh now.
* A small **cloud with a line through it** next to the app name means you are offline. You see the last saved list; pull down to try again.
* *About → Check for updated content* also refreshes.

## 7.9 About screen

Shows the app version, the **privacy policy**, terms (if any), **Contact us**, open-source licences, a refresh button and **Clear recently opened** (removes your history from the Home screen).

## 7.10 Accessibility

* Works with **TalkBack** (Android) and **VoiceOver** (iOS): every card announces its name, badge and description.
* Follows your phone's **font size** setting.
* Follows **Remove animations / Reduce motion**: sliding and animated effects stop.
* Supports dark and light appearance (chosen by the publisher, or following your phone).

## 7.11 Privacy in short

* No account, no personal data collected by the app.
* Favourites and history stay on your device.
* Websites you open are run by other organisations and have their own privacy policies.
* Full policy: **About → Privacy policy**.

## 7.12 Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Nothing loads / offline icon | Check Wi-Fi or mobile data, then pull down to refresh. |
| A channel shows an error page | Tap **Try again**. The website itself may be down; try **Open in browser**. |
| A video will not play | Some streams only play in the official app or browser: press and hold → **Open in browser**. |
| A channel disappeared | The publisher may have removed it. It also disappears from Favourites. |
| Text is too small or large | Change the font size in your phone's settings. |
| Still stuck | **About → Contact us**. |
